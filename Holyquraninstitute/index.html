Our Sacred Quranic Courses

Structured step-by-step courses designed for students of every age and background—from beginner phonetics to advanced recitation and memorization.

Noorani Qaida

Beginners & Children (Age 4+)

The essential gateway course for beginners. Learn the Arabic alphabet, correct pronunciation points (Makharij), joining letters, vowels, Sukoon, and Tashdeed to read the Quran with ease.

  • Arabic Alphabet Recognition & Sound Rules
  • Harakat (Fatha, Kasra, Damma) & Tanween
  • Connecting Compound Letters & Madd Letters
  • Sukoon, Tashdeed & Foundational Tajweed

Core Recitation

Quran Reading

Children, Teens & Adults

Designed for students who have finished Noorani Qaida and want to read the Holy Quran fluently. Focuses on continuous recitation, correct pausing (Waqf), and building independent reading confidence.

  • Fluent Nazra Recitation from Juz 1 to 30
  • Rules of Stopping Signs (Waqf, Meem, Laa)
  • Rhythmic Recitation & Breath Control
  • Word-by-word Teacher Correction & Repetition

Mastery

Quran with Tajweed

Intermediate to Advanced Learners

Learn to recite the Quran exactly as revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Deep dive into articulation points (Makharij), characteristics of letters (Sifaat), Ahkam Nun & Meem Sakin, and elongation rules (Madd).

  • 17 Specific Makharij (Throat, Tongue, Lips)
  • Ahkam Nun Sakinah & Tanween (Izhar, Idgham, Ikhfa, Iqlab)
  • Qalqalah, Ghunnah, Raa & Laam Heavy/Light Rules
  • Types of Madd (Natural, Obligatory, Permissible)

  • Personalized Daily Memorization Plan
  • Rigorous Daily Sabqi & Manzil Retention
  • Mutashabihat (Similar Verses) Guidance
