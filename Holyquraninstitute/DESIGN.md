## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies reverent scholarship, academic precision, and architectural balance. It rejects ornate cliches, choosing instead a modern editorial aesthetic rooted in restrained Islamic geometry, subtle proportions, and deliberate luxury. The design speaks to students, parents, and scholars seeking an elevated, authentic environment for sacred study.

Key attributes:
- **Tone:** Dignified, scholarly, serene, and modern.
- **Visual Rhythm:** Generous vertical pacing, high contrast between dark regal navy and warm metallic gold, paired with calm ivory canvas areas.
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Editorial academic minimalism elevated by micro-details—hairline decorative rules, quiet gold chamfers, and balanced geometric framing.

## Colors

The palette establishes an atmosphere of nocturnal clarity and luminous gold leaf. Deep oceanic navies replace conventional forest tones, evoking depth and contemplation.

### Palette Tokens
- **Midnight Canvas & Primary Text (`#0A192F` / `#0B192C`):** Used for solid brand blocks, dominant headings, and deep evening interfaces.
- **Academy Navy (`#0F2546`):** The foundational primary surface and brand anchor.
- **Regal Blue (`#1A365D`):** Interactive active states, secondary container backgrounds, and tinted overlays.
- **Polished Gold (`#C5A059`):** Accent gold for active indicators, refined iconography, focal highlights, and borders.
- **Radiant Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Primary interactive CTA fill, key trust marks, and dynamic hover focus.
- **Champagne Tint (`#F3E5AB` / `#E8D39E`):** Delicate secondary fills, highlight glow backdrops, and active tab highlights.
- **Sacred Canvas / Ivory (`#FDFBF7`):** Warm background base providing an organic parchment feel without yellowing.
- **Cool Surface Tint (`#F8FAFC`):** Alternating table rows, subtle card panels, and utility backgrounds.
- **Dividers & Structural Slate (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline outlines and neutral structural boundaries.
- **Charcoal Slate (`#334155` / `#475569`):** Body text delivering high legibility and soft contrast against ivory surfaces.

## Typography

Typography balances historical gravitas with razor-sharp interface functionality. 

- **Display & Headings:** `Playfair Display` provides high-contrast, editorial elegance reminiscent of classical book printing and traditional Quranic commentary title pages. Set with slight negative tracking on large scales to command authority.
- **Body & Controls:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric clarity, open apertures, and exceptional legibility across dense curricula schedules, fee tables, and reading interfaces.
- **Arabic / Script Integration:** When rendering Quranic script or terms, pair with traditional Naskh or Amiri stylings at a 1.25x scale factor relative to neighboring Latin text to maintain optical balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an anchored 12-column grid system paired with strict geometric rhythm. Sections breathe through calculated vertical margins, reflecting tranquility and scholarly contemplation.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4-column layout, 1.25rem margin, 1rem gutter)
  - Tablet: `768px – 1023px` (8-column layout, 2rem margin, 1.5rem gutter)
  - Desktop: `1024px – 1440px` (12-column layout, max-width 1280px, 3rem margin, 1.5rem gutter)
  - Wide Display: `> 1440px` (Centered stage, max content container 1360px)
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt base unit. Card padding is generous, enforcing visual composure. Elements never sit edge-to-edge; margins isolate callouts like illuminated manuscripts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive blur or stark drop shadows. Instead, it relies on tonal layers, hairline boundary definition, and gentle ambient occlusion tinted with navy and umber.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral `#FDFBF7` surface, zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Border `1px solid #E2E8F0` with ambient shadow `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Card Focus):** Border `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)` with lift shadow `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.03)`. Micro-translation of `-2px` on hover.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Floating Navigation Header):** Background `#FFFFFF` with 95% opacity blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), bordered with `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, cast with `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(10, 25, 47, 0.12)`.
- **Metallic Edge Effect:** Key elevated elements (like featured pricing tiers) incorporate a dual-border effect: a 1px solid gold outer rim backed by an inner 1px inset glow in `#F3E5AB`.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects architectural masonry and precision bookbinding. Curves are disciplined and soft (`roundedness: 1`), avoiding both clinical sharp edges and playful toy-like bubble radii.

- **Default UI (`rounded` / 4px):** Form controls, badges, buttons, fee tables, tabs.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** Course modules, testimonial cards, structured forms.
- **Speciality Panels (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Hero feature blocks and modal surfaces.
- **Geometric Motif Framing:** Decorative cards use geometric cuts: 8-point star corner brackets, stepped hairline borders, or a 45-degree micro-chamfer on badge tabs.
